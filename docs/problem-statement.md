# An AI Assistant over a Phone Call
Category: Consumer & social. Requested by Soumyadeep Mukherjee, Co-founder & CTO, Dashverse.

## The request
AI assistant over phone calls.

## How we read it (TPF's words)
Not everyone has a smartphone, a data plan or the habit of opening an app. An assistant you reach by dialling
an ordinary number would bring AI to anyone with a phone, in their own language.

## Ways in
- Call a number, ask a question in Hindi or another Indian language, and get a spoken answer.
- One useful job done end to end over a call, like checking a train's status or booking an appointment.
